## Bank outage

Id: `bank-outage`

Chidi runs Chidi Logistics, a delivery business in Port Harcourt. He pays drivers by bank transfer. The bank network fails for two minutes and every transfer returns a server error. His app must retry safely and must not pay a driver twice.

Twins: Paystack and Flutterwave.

Rules:

- Server error (`server-error`) on `transfer` with `times`: empty.

Steps:

- After 120 seconds, remove the rule `failing-transfers`.
